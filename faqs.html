❓ Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries about our school, programs, and admissions.

📝 What is the admission process? +

You can apply online or visit the school office for details. Our admissions team will guide you through the process including document submission, interaction, and enrollment.

🎓 Do you offer scholarships? +

Yes, we offer need-based and merit-based scholarships. Eligible students can apply during the admission process.

⚽ What extracurriculars are available? +

Sports, arts, music, dance, debate, science club, and student clubs are available to explore interests and talents.

🚌 Is transportation available? +

Yes, we provide safe school bus services across the city with GPS tracking and trained attendants.

⏰ What are the school timings? +

Our school runs from 9:30 AM to 3:30 PM, Sunday to Friday. Pre-school timings may vary slightly.

👧 What age groups do you accept? +

We accept children aged 3 to 16 years for our preschool, primary school, and secondary school.

👩‍🏫 What is the student-teacher ratio? +

We maintain a low student-teacher ratio of 15:1 to ensure personalized attention and quality learning.

🍎 Do you provide meals? +

Yes, healthy snacks and meals are provided as part of our daily schedule, prepared in a hygienic kitchen.

📱 How can parents stay updated? +

We communicate via our school app, emails, newsletters, and regular parent-teacher meetings.
